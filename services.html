How we work

From first chat to finished memories

Every occasion is different. Our approach is centred around understanding the moments that matter.

  1. UnderstandWe take the time to understand the occasion, the important moments and what you want captured.
  2. PrepareWe approach each assignment with attention to the requirements of the occasion and the coverage needed.
  3. CaptureWe focus on the people, emotions, details and moments that make your story unique.
  4. CreateWe carefully work with the captured photographs and video to produce memorable visual content.
  5. DeliverYour completed photographs and videos are prepared and delivered according to the selected service or package.
